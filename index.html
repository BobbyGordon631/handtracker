Air Harp

Draw strings of light with your hands. Play them by sweeping a finger through.

  • Pinch both hands, pull apart, release: place a string
  • Pinch one hand, drag, release: also places a string
  • Swipe your index finger through a string to pluck it
  • Flick fast for sparks · sweep across several to strum
  • Hold an open palm still to clear · pinch a string's middle and pull to break it
